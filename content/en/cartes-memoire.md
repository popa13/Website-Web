<h1 id="CartesMemoire">Flashcards</h1>
<p class="subtitle">MPU1054 Analysis in One Real Variable II: the definitions, theorems and examples of the course notes, section by section. The game is in French.</p>

<div class="app-barre">
<details class="app-aide">
<summary>How to use it</summary>
<div class="app-aide-contenu">
<p>The cards are the statements of the course notes, with their numbers: definitions, theorems, corollaries, lemmas and important examples of Sections 1.1 to 3.4. First choose the material: a few sections, a whole chapter or the whole course (<i>Toute la matière</i>).</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Révision</b> (review): the front shows the name of a statement, to be stated, or the statement itself, with its defined terms hidden, to be named. Flip the card and rate yourself: <i>À revoir</i> (again), <i>Hésitant</i> (hard) or <i>Je savais</i> (known). Difficult cards come back later in the session, and each card keeps a mastery level from one session to the next.</li>
<li><b>Défi éclair</b> (timed challenge): one statement, four names and a timer. Streaks of right answers earn more points, and the best score is kept for each choice of material.</li>
<li><b>Keyboard</b>: Space flips the card, 1, 2 and 3 rate it; in the challenge, 1 to 4 pick an answer and Enter moves on.</li>
</ul>
<p>Progress and records stay in the browser: nothing is sent anywhere.</p>
<p class="rd-credit">Application in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, made with the help of Claude (Anthropic) from the course notes. Formulas are typeset with KaTeX.</p>
