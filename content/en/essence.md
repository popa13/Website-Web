<h1 id="GasPrices">Gas prices in Québec</h1>
<p class="subtitle">Pump prices at every service station in Québec, on a map, from the Régie de l’énergie data.</p>

<div class="app-barre">
<details class="app-aide">
<summary>How to use</summary>
<div class="app-aide-contenu">
<p>The map is in French.</p>
<ul>
<li>Choose the fuel: <em>régulier</em> (regular), <em>super</em> or <em>diesel</em>.</li>
<li>Search for a city, an address or a brand, or move the map. The list shows the stations in view, with the lowest, average and highest price.</li>
<li>The colours compare the stations in view, from green (cheaper) to red (more expensive).</li>
<li>Limit the search to a radius of 5 to 50 km, sort by price or distance, and use the location button to place yourself on the map.</li>
<li>Click a station to see all its prices and get directions.</li>
</ul>
<p>Source: <a href="https://regieessencequebec.ca" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Régie de l’énergie du Québec</a>. Prices are reported by the retailers: check them at the pump.</p>
