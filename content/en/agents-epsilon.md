<h1 id="AgentsEpsilon">Agents ε</h1>
<p class="subtitle">MPU1054 Analysis in One Real Variable II: a team game on the notions of the course, inspired by <i>Codenames</i>. The game is in French.</p>

<div class="app-barre">
<details class="app-aide">
<summary>How to use it</summary>
<div class="app-aide-contenu">
<p>The grid, projected in class, holds 25 notions from the chosen sections (fewer if the material is small). Secretly, 9 cards belong to the team that starts, 8 to the other team, 7 are neutral and one is the <b>counterexample</b> (<i>contre-exemple</i>).</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Set up</b>: choose the material, then <i>Créer la partie</i>. The <i>Clé des chefs</i> window shows a QR code, blurred until <i>Montrer le code</i> is clicked, which the two spymasters scan with their phones to see the colour of each card.</li>
<li><b>Play</b>: in turn, a spymaster gives one word and a number; the team points at cards, which are tapped on the screen. Own card: the team goes on; neutral or opposing card: the turn ends; counterexample: the game is lost. A card tapped by mistake can be undone for a few seconds.</li>
<li><b>Review</b>: each revealed card shows the corresponding statement of the course notes, and <i>Notions de la partie</i> lists them all at the end.</li>
</ul>
<p>The board and the spymasters’ phones rebuild the same grid from the game code: there is no account and no server, and the game in progress stays in the browser until the next class.</p>
<p class="rd-credit">Application in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, made with the help of Claude (Anthropic) from the course notes. Formulas are typeset with KaTeX. Rules inspired by the game <i>Codenames</i> by Vlaada Chvátil.</p>
</div>
</details>
