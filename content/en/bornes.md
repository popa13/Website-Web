<h1 id="EVCharging">EV charging stations in Québec</h1>
<p class="subtitle">Public charging stations of every network on one map, filtered by network and power.</p>

<div class="app-barre">
<details class="app-aide">
<summary>How to use</summary>
<div class="app-aide-contenu">
<p>The map is in French.</p>
<ul>
<li>Choose the power: all stations, level 2, or fast charging at 50, 100 or 180 kW and more.</li>
<li>Search for a city, an address or a name, and pick one or more networks (Circuit électrique, FLO, Tesla, ChargePoint and the others).</li>
<li>The list shows the stations in view, sorted by power or by distance; the location button places you on the map.</li>
<li>Click a station to see its connectors and get directions.</li>
</ul>
<p>Source: the charging station locator of Natural Resources Canada. Public stations in service only. Check availability in the network’s app before leaving. A first prototype.</p>
