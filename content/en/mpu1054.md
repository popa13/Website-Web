<h1 id="MPU1054">MPU1054 – Analysis in One Real Variable II</h1>
<p class="subtitle">Fall 2026 · Université du Québec à Trois-Rivières · Department of Mathematics and Computer Science · taught in French</p>

<p>
	This course develops the theory of the Riemann integral and deepens the understanding of integral calculus and of series of functions: uniform continuity, the Riemann integral (fundamental theorem of calculus, improper integrals), sequences of functions, numerical series, series of functions and power series (Taylor series). It follows MPU1045 – Analysis in One Real Variable I, which is its prerequisite.
</p>

<p>
	The course has four chapters:
</p>
<ol>
	<li>the Riemann integral;</li>
	<li>sequences of functions;</li>
	<li>numerical series;</li>
	<li>series of functions.</li>
</ol>
